# Section 2 food — Storlien → Gäddede

**You only.** Companion stops in Storlien. **15–28 Feb 2027** · D11–D24 · **14 ski days**.  
Days: [dag-for-dag-2027.md](./dag-for-dag-2027.md). §1 menu: [food-section1-2027.md](./food-section1-2027.md). Shopping: [food-shopping-2027.md](./food-shopping-2027.md).

**Locked**

- The NL box rides in the car to **Grövelsjön**, then is **posted to Storlien** before the ski. It is not on the §1 pulk.
- Collect it in Storlien on **13–14 Feb**. Send it to yourself at **Eurocash / Fjellhandel** (PostNord ombud), or to **Högfjällshotell** if they confirm they will hold it. Phone before you post.
- **Additional meals are bought in Storlien** (Coop / Eurocash).
- **Companion leftovers** in Storlien go on your pulk: whatever they have not eaten. No fixed weight.
- **Fuel:** buy **2 L** alkylate in Storlien. No canisters.

## Bought in Storlien

The box does not cover every breakfast or every dinner on the 14 days. Buy the rest at **Coop / Eurocash** on 13–14 Feb: the other breakfasts, and the dinners past the 5 freeze-dried meals and the pasta and rice.

**Valsjöbua** (day 21) can still top up food on the way. It is not required for leaving Storlien.

---

## Companion leftovers

Handed over in Storlien before they leave on **14 Feb**. Add them to the pulk with the shop food. Typical leftovers are unused Cruesli, bars, nuts, dried fruit, or an uneaten dinner. Eat those first.

---

## If the pulk is too heavy

Post the surplus from **Storlien** to **Gäddede** on 13–14 Feb and ski with the lighter load. Collect it on **28 Feb**.

Send it to **Pilgrimshotellet** (they take a free depot) or to **ICA Gäddede** (PostNord). Phone before you post. Fuel stays on the pulk.
