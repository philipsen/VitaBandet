## Route & logistics

- **Planned direction:** Grövelsjön → Treriksröset  
- **Daily distance target (km):** ramp **10 → 23 km**; cruise **~20–23 km** (~22 km winter average); see [dag-for-dag](./dag-for-dag-2027.md)  
- **Rest / weather buffer:** no fixed **R** days — resupply stops only; **Apr 20–30** = ~11 spare days  
- **Resupply strategy:** **local shops first** (see [resupply-2027.md](./resupply-2027.md)); depots only for special items  
- **Fuel:** **Alkylaatbenzine** in bottles only until **Abisko** (white-gas jet + silencer); **no gas canisters** before day 61 — see [resupply alkylate list](./resupply-2027.md)  
- **Kvikkjokk → Abisko:** Padjelanta-west → **Ritsem** → **KL** via **Sälka** → Abisko ([dag-for-dag](./dag-for-dag-2027.md))  
- **First major shop:** **Storlien** — see [dag-for-dag](./dag-for-dag-2027.md) · GPX ref `tracks/source/lottas-och-bjorns-band.gpx`
- **Accommodation mix:** **mostly tent**; planned **H** at **Storlien**, Gäddede, Hemavan, Kvikkjokk, **Ritsem**, Kungsleden huts, **Lappjord / Altevass (DNT)**, Pältsa (see [planned hut nights](./dag-for-dag-2027.md))  
- **DNT key** required for Norwegian huts after Abisko (Lappjordhytta, Altevasshytta, Gaskashytta, Rostahytta)  
- **Border / permit notes:** *(if relevant)*  
- **Waypoints:** [VGB regler](https://www.vitagronabandet.se/infor-turen/regler/) — six places; **väster om** (S→N = point **east** of track). Wide corridor OK. ICA at **D** = resupply.
