## Grövelsjön renovation (winter 2027)

[STF Grövelsjön](https://www.svenskaturistforeningen.se/boende/stf-grovelsjon-fjallstation/) · winter packages confirm:

- **Open:** 14 Jan – 18 Apr 2027 (rooms, self-cater kitchen, a shop).
- **Closed:** restaurant, café, **sauna**. Kitchen renovation from **Oct 2026**.
- **Eat:** Sjöstugan · Storsätern · Lövåsen · Grövelfjäll värdshus · Fjällbageri at Grövelsjögården.
- **Shop:** Fjällbua (Storsätern) is still the full livs. Do not count on the STF kiosk for the §1 load.
