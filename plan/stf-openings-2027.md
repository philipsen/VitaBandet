## Grövelsjön renovation (winter 2027)

[STF Grövelsjön](https://www.svenskaturistforeningen.se/boende/stf-grovelsjon-fjallstation/) · confirmed 26 Nov 2026:

- **Open:** 14 Jan – 18 Apr 2027 (rooms, self-cater kitchen, shop, rental). Reception and shop **08:30–19:30**. Winter rental **08:30–12:00** and **16:00–18:00**.
- **Closed:** restaurant, café, **sauna** — through winter and through summer **24 Jun – 3 Oct 2027**. They reopen before Christmas 2027.
- **Shop:** open, with a wider food range (frozen, chilled, vegetables, dry goods) plus fika and outdoor kit. **Fjällbua** (Storsätern) is still the village livs. §1 food stays the NL carry.
- **Eat:** Sjöstugan · Storsätern · Lövåsen · Grövelfjäll värdshus · Fjällbageri at Grövelsjögården.
- **Discount:** 10% on lodging with code **STFMEDLEM**, 14 Jan – 3 Oct 2027.
