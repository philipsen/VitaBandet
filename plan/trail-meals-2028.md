# Vita Bandet 2028 — trail meals (breakfast + 29 dinners)

**Solo · pot + pan · fuel-conscious · shop along the route**

Breakfast and dinners for camp cooking on **Optimus OmniLite** + **TOAKS 1600 ml** (pot) with **pan/lid** (~142 mm fry pan). **Lunch:** mostly **bars + nötter** on ski days; **Polarbröd/knäcke + salami** when you want a change. Dinners aim at **one boil + short simmer** (or soak-only where noted). Portions are **one hungry skier** (~600–900 kcal per dinner before snacks); add **olive oil, cheese, or nuts** if you need more. Target **~4,000–5,000 kcal/day** total with lunch snacks ([packing-pulk](./packing-pulk-2028.md)).

**Shops:** ICA Nära / Coop / Eurocash / Handlar'n / STF butik — staples below are normal Swedish/Norwegian grocery stock (Feb–Apr). Village livs may lack fresh herbs or fancy cheese; substitutes noted.

Related: [resupply-2028.md](./resupply-2028.md) · [packing-pulk-2028.md](./packing-pulk-2028.md) · [gear-inventory-2028.md](./gear-inventory-2028.md)

## Cooking habits (save fuel)

| Habit | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Soak** pasta / rice / couscous in cold water in the pot while pitching tent | Cuts boil time 30–50 % |
| **Pan first** — brown onion, korv, bacon in the pan, then deglaze into pot | Flavour without extra fuel |
| **Lid on** (use pan as lid) | Faster boil, less evaporation |
| **Simmer low** on OmniLite; titanium burns fast — watch the bottom |
| **Pan + fat** — uncoated Ti **sticks** (pannkakor, eggs); use **extra smör/olja**, low heat, wait until edges lift before flipping |
| **Freeze-dried as base** only when you want zero fuss — still add oil + shop carbs |

**Fuel target:** aim for **~15–25 g alkylate per dinner** after shakedown (melt water is separate).

## Breakfast

Default **daily** breakfast; **pannkakor** as a treat **1–2 mornings per resupply leg** when you have fresh **ägg** + **smör** and are not on a low-fuel leg (skip Padjelanta).

### Daily — crunchy müsli + mjölkpulver

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★☆☆ — hot water only (often same boil as tea) |
| **Shop** | **Mjölkpulver** + **müsli** at ICA, Coop, Handlar'n; **not** Quaker Cruesli (expat shops only) |

**Ingredients:** crunchy **müsli** 60–80 g · **mjölkpulver** ~15 g · hot water ~400 ml

**Method:** Reconstitute mjölkpulver in mug with hot melt water. Pour over müsli. Eat from mug/bowl.

**Pack:** müsli + mjölkpulver in **Box 2 (lunches)**; keep powder dry. One **480 g Semper** or **400 g Nido** pack lasts ~8–10 resupply legs.

**Alternate:** **havregryn** + mjölkpulver + smör/honung — same fuel rating.

**Zero-cook:** müsli + cold water + mjölkpulver works; less pleasant in **Feb** cold.

**Per leg carry:** ~70 g müsli × days + mjölkpulver as needed; optional **1 bag pannkaksmix** (~200 g) for treat mornings.

### Treat — pannkakor i TOAKS-panna

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — pan preheat + 2–3 thin pannkakor (~10 min) |
| **Shop** | **Pannkaksmix** (Kungsörnen / ICA / Felix) everywhere; **ägg** + **smör** from shop — fine for several days in cold |

**Ingredients:** **pannkaksmix** 100 g · **ägg** 1 · **mjölk** 250 ml (or mjölkpulver + water) · **smör** 1 msk per batch · **sylt** / lingon

**Method:** Mix batter thin. **Low simmer** on OmniLite — Ti heats fast. Generous **smör** in **panna** (~142 mm — one small pannkaka at a time). Flip when edges lift; first pancake often sticks (sacrificial). Cook on flat stone/sit pad in vestibule.

**When:** morning after Storlien, Valsjöbua, Gäddede, Hemavan, Ritsem, Abisko — not on Padjelanta fuel-critical days. **ägg** + **smör** keep well in the pulk in **Feb–Mar** cold.

## Lunch — bars & nötter (default)

**Zero fuel** — eat on short ski breaks; no stove stop. Pack **today’s lunch** in **Box 3** (top of stack); bulk in **Box 2**.

### Typical ski day

| Item | Amount | Shop (Swedish labels) |
|------|--------|------------------------|
| **Nötter** | 1 handful (~40 g) | **cashew**, **mandel**, **jordnötter**, **nötmix** — ICA påse or bulk |
| **Chokladbars** | **2** | **Mars**, **Snickers**, **Bounty**, **Twix**, **Daim**, **KitKat** — all standard at ICA/Coop |
| **Müsli- / granolabars** | **2** | **Nature Valley**, **Friskis**, **Eat Natural**, **ProPud**, ICA/Coop own — look for **müslibar** / **granola bar** |

**~900–1,100 kcal** lunch before dinner — pairs with müsli breakfast and cooked dinner for daily target.

**Box 3 (today):** 4 bars + nuts bag for the day — refill each evening from Box 2.

**Per 10-day leg:** ~20 chokladbars + ~20 müslibars + **~400 g nötter** (one large påse or two small). Buy mixed multipacks at **D** shops to save repacking.

## Lunch — bröd & salami (variation)

Swap bar days for **bröd + pålägg** when you want something savoury — especially right after a shop when **salami** / **ost** are fresh.

| Bread | Shop label | Trail notes |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Polarbröd** | **Jubileumsbröd**, **Tunna bröd**, **Pitabröd** | Soft flat rounds — **Norrland staple**. Unopened bag keeps **weeks** in cold. |
| **Knäckebröd** | **Wasa**, **Leksands**, **Krisprolls** | Shelf-stable — good when Polarbröd bag is empty. |

**Bröd lunch:** 2–3 **Polarbröd** or **knäcke** · **salami** / **skinka** / **renskav** · **ost** · smör/Bregott · optional **tomat** / **gurka** (first days after shop)

**Mix through the leg:** e.g. **5–6 bar days** + **3–4 bröd days** per 10-day carry — or all bars on Padjelanta when bread weight matters less than simplicity.

**Per leg carry (if mixing):** 1 **Polarbröd** bag + **salami** stick (200–400 g) + **ost** block; knäcke pack lasts multiple legs.

**Dinner use:** knäcke for **#11** shakshuka · **#20** grilled cheese · **#23** pytt — **#25** pannpizza uses **Polarbröd**; bröd bought for lunch can double for those nights.

## Fresh & frozen meat (cold trail)

**Feb–Apr on ski:** the pulk is an outdoor freezer most of the trip. Prefer **shop frozen** over chilled when ICA/Coop has both — it lasts longer and thaws slowly while skiing.

| Source | Examples (Swedish labels) | Carry window (typical) |
|--------|---------------------------|------------------------|
| **Frozen** (ICA/Coop freezer) | **grillkorv**, **kycklingfilé**, **köttfärs**, **bacon** (frozen pack), **fläskfilé**, **fryst färdigmat** | **~5–7 days** Feb–Mar while pulk stays ≤ 0 °C; shorter if mild Apr days |
| **Chilled** (same shops) | **ätklar kyckling**, **korv**, **bacon**, **nötfärs** | **~3–5 days** in cold; treat like frozen if nights are hard frost |
| **STF Large** (Ritsem, Kvikkjokk) | Chilled **ägg**, **smör**, **korv**, **pizza**, frozen ready meals | Top-up on hut-shop days |
| **Burk** (tail of leg / warm spell) | Corned beef, skinka, pilsnerkorv, köttbullar | Until opened — backup when fresh is used or thawed |

**Leg strategy**

1. At each **D** shop: buy **2–4 dinners** of **frozen meat** + fresh **ägg** / **grädde** / **lök** — eat these **first** on the leg (best flavour, less tin weight).
2. Carry **2–3 meat tins** per leg for days **6+** or if weather turns mild.
3. **Mid-leg top-ups** (Hamra D4, Valsjöbua D20, Klimpfjäll D28, Ammarnäs D38): re-buy frozen meat on long Storlien→Gäddede or Gäddede→Hemavan carries.
4. **Pack:** frozen flat in **Box 1** (dinners), bottom of pulk — coldest spot. **Never** next to fuel bottles.

**Recipes that shine with fresh/frozen:** **#2** bacon/skinka · **#4** ägg + korv · **#7** grillkorv · **#11–12** ägg/bacon · **#14** tortellini · **#21–25** köttfärs/korv/kyckling · **#26** renskav · **#27–28** köttfärs/korv.

**When to rely on tins:** Padjelanta (no shops, long carry) · village livs with thin stock · **Apr** legs if daytime temps go above ~+5 °C for several days.

Plain **canned chicken breast** is **not** standard in Swedish shops. Use these instead:

| **Bullens pilsnerkorv** (455 g) | Ambient tin | Slice, fry — pasta, breakfast |
| **Felix köttbullar i gräddsås** (560 g) | Ready-meal tin | Heat in pot 5 min — zero fuss |

| **Luncheon fläsk** (Gavrilovic) | Spam-style tin | Fry with eggs/onion |

**Prefer fresh/frozen from shop when cold** — see [Fresh & frozen meat](#fresh--frozen-meat-cold-trail) above. Tins are **backup**, not the default at every major **D**.

## The 29 dinners

### 1. Couscous med corned beef & lök

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★☆☆ — pour boiling water, soak 5 min |
| **Shop** | **Corned beef** (burk) anywhere; early in leg: **grillkorv** / **köttfärs** (fryst) instead |

**Ingredients:** couscous 80 g · **corned beef** ½ burk **or** **grillkorv** 1 / **köttfärs** 150 g (fryst/färsk) · lök · olivolja · **citronpeppar** · salt · ev. **kapris** / pepparrot

**Method:** Stek kött/korv/färs i **panna** med hackad lök 2–3 min. Koka vatten i **gryta**. Häll kokande vatten över couscous (1:1), lock 5 min. Blanda i grytan. Ät ur grytan.

### 2. Snabb pasta aglio e olio (+ skinka eller bacon)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — blöt pasta, kort kok |
| **Shop** | Everywhere |

**Ingredients:** pasta 90 g · vitlök 2 klyftor · olivolja 2 msk · **chiliflakes** · **skinka** tärnad / **bacon** 50 g / **pilsnerkorv** skivad · parmesan/riven ost om finns

**Method:** Blöt pasta i kallt vatten i grytan 20–30 min. Stek skinka/bacon/korv i panna, ta upp. Hetta olja, svag fräs vitlök (bränn inte). Koka pasta 3–4 min i grytan, spara lite vatten. Blanda pasta i panna med olja + stärkelsevatten + kött.

---

### 3. Svamprisotto (snabb variant)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — 18–20 min simmer med lock |
| **Shop** | ICA+ har **torkad svamp**; annars **frysta svamp** / bara buljong |

**Ingredients:** ris 80 g · torkad svamp eller 1 dl fryst · lök · **grönsaksbuljong** · smör/olja · riven ost

**Method:** Fräs lök + svamp i panna. Skaka ris i panna 1 min. Flytta till gryta, tillsätt ~400 ml buljong (het). Simma tätt 18 min, rör sällan. Ost sist.

### 4. Upgraded snabbnudlar (ägg & grönt)

| | |

**Ingredients:** **snabbnudlar** 1 förp · **ägg** 1–2 · **frysta wokgrönsaker** 1 dl · **soja** · sesamolja om finns · ev. tofu/korv

**Method:** Koka nudlar i gryta 2 min, tillsätt fryst grönt. Skiva ägg i panna med lite olja (omelettbitar). Häll av nudlar, blanda i panna med soja.

## Meal plan tips on trail

| Situation | Suggestion |
|-----------|------------|
| **Daily breakfast** | **Müsli + mjölkpulver** — zero fuss; pack in Box 2 |
| **Daily lunch (skiing)** | **Default:** nötter + **2 chokladbars** + **2 müslibars** in Box 3 · **Variation:** Polarbröd/knäcke + **salami** after shop |
| **Treat breakfast** | **Pannkakor** 1–2× per leg after big shop (ägg + smör) — skip Padjelanta |
| **First half of leg (cold)** | **Frozen/fresh meat** — korv, bacon, kyckling, köttfärs (7, 11–12, 14, 21–25, 27–28) |
| **Second half / mild Apr** | **Burk**, **torkat**, **bulgur/couscous** (1, 5, 9, 10, 15, 21, 27 with corned beef) |
| **Small village livs** | 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 13, 20, 21, 28, 29 — shelf-stable |
| **After 10 h skiing** | 4, 15, 10, **26**, **29** — minimal fuss |
| **Low fuel leg** (Padjelanta) | **1, 9, 10, 15, 17, 26, 29** (★☆☆); müsli breakfast |
| **Norrland bonus** | **#17** lax/ren · **#26** renskav (Hemavan → Kvikkjokk) |
| **Polarbröd at dinner** | **#25** pannpizza — bröd/salami bought for lunch can cover this too |

Rotate **protein** (**frozen/fresh meat** / meat tin / beans / egg / frystorkat / ren) — no default fish.

## Pack per day (rough)

| Item | Weight |
|------|--------|
| Breakfast (müsli + mjölkpulver) | ~75–95 g |
| Lunch — bars + nötter (default day) | ~200–280 g |
| Lunch — bröd + salami (variation day) | ~150–220 g |
| Dry carbs (dinner) | 80–100 g |
| Protein (dry/tinned **or** frozen portion) | 80–200 g |
| Oil / sauce | 15–30 g |
| **Total dry per day** | **~370–475 g** × 10 days ≈ **3.7–4.8 kg** food only |

At each **D** shop: buy **frozen/fresh meat** for the first half of the leg (pulk stays cold); **tins and dry** for the rest. **ägg**, **lök**, **smör**, **grädde** all keep longer than summer hiking in **Feb–Mar**.
