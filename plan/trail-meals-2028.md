# Vita Bandet 2028 — trail dinners (20 recipes)

**Solo · pot + pan · fuel-conscious · shop along the route**

Dinners for camp cooking on **Optimus OmniLite** + **TOAKS 1600 ml** (pot) with **pan/lid**. Aimed at **one boil + short simmer** (or soak-only where noted). Portions are **one hungry skier** (~600–900 kcal per meal before snacks); add **olive oil, cheese, or nuts** if you need more.

**Shops:** ICA Nära / Coop / Eurocash / Handlar'n / STF butik — staples below are normal Swedish/Norwegian grocery stock (Feb–Apr). Village livs may lack fresh herbs or fancy cheese; substitutes noted.

Related: [resupply-2028.md](./resupply-2028.md) · [packing-pulk-2028.md](./packing-pulk-2028.md) · [gear-inventory-2028.md](./gear-inventory-2028.md)

## Cooking habits (save fuel)

| Habit | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Soak** pasta / rice / couscous in cold water in the pot while pitching tent | Cuts boil time 30–50 % |
| **Pan first** — brown onion, korv, bacon in the pan, then deglaze into pot | Flavour without extra fuel |
| **Lid on** (use pan as lid) | Faster boil, less evaporation |
| **Simmer low** on OmniLite; titanium burns fast — watch the bottom |
| **Freeze-dried as base** only when you want zero fuss — still add oil + shop carbs |

**Fuel target:** aim for **~15–25 g alkylate per dinner** after shakedown (melt water is separate).

## The 20 dinners

### 1. Couscous med tonfisk & citron

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★☆☆ — pour boiling water, soak 5 min |
| **Shop** | Everywhere |

**Ingredients:** couscous 80 g · tonfisk i olja 1 burk · citron (färsk eller **citronpeppar**) · lök · olivolja · salt · ev. **kapris** / pepparrot

**Method:** Finhacka lök. Hetta olja i **panna**, fräs lök 2 min. Koka vatten i **gryta** (mindre mängd). Häll kokande vatten över couscous (1:1), lock 5 min. Blanda tonfisk, lök, olja, kryddor. Ät ur grytan.

---

### 2. Snabb pasta aglio e olio (+ tonfisk)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — blöt pasta, kort kok |
| **Shop** | Everywhere |

**Ingredients:** pasta 90 g · vitlök 2 klyftor · olivolja 2 msk · **chiliflakes** · tonfisk (valfritt) · parmesan/riven ost om finns

**Method:** Blöt pasta i kallt vatten i grytan 20–30 min. Hetta olja i panna, svag fräs vitlök (bränn inte). Koka pasta 3–4 min i grytan, spara lite vatten. Blanda pasta i panna med olja + stärkelsevatten. Tonfisk sist.

---

### 3. Svamprisotto (snabb variant)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — 18–20 min simmer med lock |
| **Shop** | ICA+ har **torkad svamp**; annars **frysta svamp** / bara buljong |

**Ingredients:** ris 80 g · torkad svamp eller 1 dl fryst · lök · **grönsaksbuljong** · smör/olja · riven ost

**Method:** Fräs lök + svamp i panna. Skaka ris i panna 1 min. Flytta till gryta, tillsätt ~400 ml buljong (het). Simma tätt 18 min, rör sällan. Ost sist.

### 4. Upgraded snabbnudlar (ägg & grönt)

| | |
|--|--|

**Ingredients:** **snabbnudlar** 1 förp · **ägg** 1–2 · **frysta wokgrönsaker** 1 dl · **soja** · sesamolja om finns · ev. tofu/korv

### 5. Röda linser & morotssoppa

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — 12–15 min |

### 6. Kikärts-curry (en gryta)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Fuel** | ★★☆ — 10 min |
| **Shop** | Everywhere (burk + **kokosmjölk**) |

**Ingredients:** **kikärtor** 1 burk · **kokosmjölk** 200 ml · **currypasta** (grön/röd) · lök · ris eller couscous vid sidan

**Method:** Fräs lök + currypasta i panna. Tillsätt kikärtor + kokosmjölk i **gryta**, simma 8 min. Under tiden: snabbkokt ris/couscous i separat omgång **eller** förkok ris i samma gryta först (mer fuel, en disk).

## Meal plan tips on trail

| Situation | Suggestion |
|-----------|------------|
| **First 2 nights after shop** | Meals with **ägg**, **grädde**, **färsk korv** (7, 11, 12, 14) |
| **Day 5–8 of leg** | **Burk**, **torkat**, **bulgur/couscous** (1, 5, 9, 10, 15) |
| **Small village livs** | 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 13, 20 — all shelf-stable |
| **After 10 h skiing** | 4, 15, 10 — minimal fuss |
| **Low fuel leg** (Padjelanta) | Prioritise ★☆☆ meals; soak carbs at lunch |

Rotate **protein** (fish / beans / egg / korv / frystorkat) so you do not eat tuna every night.

| Item | Weight |
|------|--------|
| Dry carbs | 80–100 g |
