# March 2027 practice trip — STF hut-to-hut (Kungsleden Jämtland)

**Goal:** 10 days on snow with **reliable nights in staffed STF fjällstugor**, ski + pulk, **not** following the Vita Bandet track. Overlaps **2028 Section 1** terrain (Helags / Sylarna / Blåhammaren / Storlien).

**Not:** full Bandet registration or waypoint compliance — training only, not a VGB expedition.

## Why this route (not Band Section 1)

On the Band track west of Helags/Sylarna there are **two–three nights with no hut on the line** (e.g. Ramundberget, Fältjägaren). STF stations sit **east** of the Band track.

On **Kungsleden**, winter huts are **on the route** — each evening a booked **H** with kitchen, drying room, and (usually) shop.

| Night | STF / town |
|-------|------------|
| 0–1 | **Storulvån** |
| 2–3 | **Helags** |
| 4–5 | **Sylarna** |
| 6 | **Blåhammaren** |
| 7–9 | **Storlien** (fjällgård / hotel) |
| 10 | Travel day / buffer |

## STF huts — booking & expectations

| Hut | Role | Winter (proxy 2026/28) | Action |
|-----|------|------------------------|--------|
| **[Storulvån](https://www.stf.se/storulvan/)** | Start + acclimatise | Warden **~19 Feb – Apr** | Book **days 0–1**; restaurant/shop |
| **[Helags](https://www.stf.se/helags/)** | Highest fjäll | Warden **~21 Feb – Apr** | Book **2 nights** (buffer day 3) |
| **[Sylarna](https://www.stf.se/sylarna/)** | High plateau | Warden **~20 Feb – Apr** | Book **2 nights** (buffer day 5) |
| **[Blåhammaren](https://www.stf.se/blahammaren/)** | Before Storlien | Warden **~20 Feb – Apr** | Restaurant **closed since 2024** — shop/self-catering |
| **Storlien** | Finish + major shop | Village hotels / **[STF fjällgård](https://www.stf.se/storlien/)** | Coop · Fjellhandeln · ask for alkylate |

- **STF membership** — discount and sometimes priority.
- **Confirm 2027 opening dates** with STF (do **not** rely on this doc alone).
- **Avalanche kit:** same as 2028 plan — moderate terrain, avoid steep alpine slopes.
- **Emergency tent** + sleeping bag: mandatory backup (storm, full hut, cancelled warden).

**If fully booked:** **STF Vålådalen** (~30 km east of Band line) — bus/taxi from Mora/Åre; extra travel day.

## 10 days on snow (draft)

*Pace **12–20 km/day** — slower than 2028 cruise; two buffer nights in huts.*

| Day | Example | km (approx.) | Route | Overnight |
|-----|---------|--------------|--------|-------------|
| **0** | Sat — travel | — | NL → **Mora/Åre** → bus to **Storulvån** | **STF Storulvån** |
| **1** | Sun | 8–12 | Acclimatise around Storulvån / Vålådalen trails | **STF Storulvån** |
| **2** | Mon | 15–18 | Storulvån → **Helags** (KL) | **STF Helags** |
| **3** | Tue | 0–10 | **R** in bad weather, or short tour; dry gear | **STF Helags** |
| **4** | Wed | 18–22 | Helags → **Sylarna** | **STF Sylarna** |
| **5** | Thu | 0–15 | **R** or Sylarna → Blåhammaren (if day 4 was hard) | **Sylarna** or **Blåhammaren** |
| **6** | Fri | 12–18 | Sylarna → **Blåhammaren** (if not done on day 5) | **STF Blåhammaren** |
| **7** | Sat | 12–14 | Blåhammaren → **Storlien** (descent) | **Storlien** (hotel/fjällgård) |
| **8** | Sun | 0 | **D** Storlien — Coop, Fjellhandeln, pulk weigh-in, alkylate | **Storlien** |
| **9** | Mon | 0–15 | Optional day tour from Storlien, or extra **R** | **Storlien** |
| **10** | Tue | — | Bus/train back to NL | — |

**Minimum ski km between huts:** ~**57 km** (days 2+4+6+7); with days 1/3/5/9 ~**65–75 km**.

**GPX:** use official **Kungsleden winter** (STF / Lantmäteriet / OsmAnd); not `tracks/2028.gpx` (Band west of stations). Optionally compare with Section 1 in [band-tracks-comparison.md](./band-tracks-comparison.md).

## Packing & consumption

### Food

| Segment | Days | Strategy |
|---------|------|----------|
| Storulvån → Storlien | 6–7 on trail | **2–3 days** in pulk between huts; top up at STF shop (expensive) |
| Storlien (day 8) | — | Practice **major resupply** like 2028 day 10: **4–5 days** food + snack test |

No need to carry 8–9 days from Grövelsjön — benefit of hut-to-hut.

### Fuel (alkylate)

- Start with **full 1 L bottles** from home or Stockholm.
- At **Storlien** (day 8): ask for **alkylbensin / Power Fuel** — see [resupply-2028.md](./resupply-2028.md) day 10.
- STF shops en route: mainly **snacks**; **no gas canisters** (same rule as 2028).

### Pulk

- **Lighter** load than 2028 Section 1 start (shorter carries).
- Still practice: **hut days** (less hanging to dry) vs one planned **tent night** backup (STF tält + service fee at Sylarna/Blåhammaren optional).

## Public transport (Netherlands)

*Check timetables **~3 months** before departure (SJ, Snälltåget, Länstrafiken Jämtland, Vy).*

### Outbound

1. **Train NL → Stockholm** (via Hamburg or Copenhagen; night train possible).
2. **SJ Stockholm → Mora** or **→ Duved/Åre** (Snälltåget to Mora often relevant in winter).
3. **Bus / taxi** to **STF Storulvån** (via **Vålådalen** / Undersåker — allow buffer in snow).
4. Optional: train to **Östersund** + bus west; longer but sometimes more flexible.

### Return (from Storlien)

1. **Train from Storlien** — border station; connections to **Åre**, **Trondheim** (Vy), sometimes **Östersund**.
2. **Via Trondheim/Oslo** or **Åre → Stockholm** back to NL.

**Fallback:** end in **Åre** (larger hub) in bad weather — taxi from Storlien area.

**Useful:** [sj.se](https://www.sj.se) · [snalltaget.se](https://www.snalltaget.se) · [lanstrafikenjamtland.se](https://www.lanstrafikenjamtland.se) · [entur.no](https://entur.no) (Norway).
