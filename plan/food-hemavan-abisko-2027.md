# Food — Hemavan → Abisko

**Buy in Hemavan** on **12–13 Mar**. This food lasts until **Abisko** (10 Apr). The Norway leg is a separate shop there.  
Days: [dag-for-dag-2027.md](./dag-for-dag-2027.md).

**Locked**

- At **ICA Nära Fjällboden**, buy **freeze-dried meals**, **breakfast**, and **lunch** for Hemavan → Abisko.
- **Ritsem → Abisko food is posted from Hemavan to STF Ritsem.** Do not buy that leg at the Ritsem shop.
- A second parcel goes to **STF Kvikkjokk** for the food you eat until Ritsem. Carry only Hemavan → Kvikkjokk.
- Collect Kvikkjokk on **26–27 Mar**. Collect the Ritsem → Abisko box on **3 Apr**.

Post from the ICA ombud. Address each box to yourself at the STF station and phone before you send it. Fuel stays on the pulk until Ritsem. From Ritsem to Abisko the nights are in staffed huts, so the stove uses **very little** alkylate.
