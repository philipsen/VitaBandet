#### Pulk / skis — will the bus take them?

| Item | Rule | Practical |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Skis** | Allowed **om plats finns** · landsbygdsbuss → **godsutrymme** · pack/bundle or ski bag · **driver decides** | OK if packed |
| **Stort bagage** (pulk counts) | Allowed **only if space** in luggage hold · else ship another way | **Not guaranteed** |
| **2× pulk** | Same hold as everyone else’s skis | **Highest risk** of refusal on a busy winter day |
| Extra cost | None for luggage/skis | — |

Official: [Vad och vem får du ta med](https://www.dalatrafik.se/sa-reser-du-med-oss/ombord/vad-och-vem-far-du-ta-med/) · [Resevillkor §7 Bagage](https://www.dalatrafik.se/kundservice/info/vara-resevillkor/). Pulk is **not named**; treat as **stort bagage**. **Call Dalatrafik 0771-95 95 95** before travel and ask specifically about **2 pulkor** on **396**.

**Prep:** empty / soft-pack pulks · ski bags · arrive early at Mora resecentrum · first-come for hold space. Prefer **midweek / early departure**.

**Alkylate:** resevillkor ban **brandfarliga** liquids — **do not** put fuel bottles on the bus. Leave full bottles in the **parked car** (companion retrieves) **or** buy/fill at Grövelsjön after arrival.

## From finish — Treriksröset (~25 Apr)

**There is no road at the cairn.** You ski out. Goal day is **not** “home same evening.” **Car is already home** (companion) — you take **train/bus south** from the north.

### Exit A ★ — Kilpisjärvi (Finland)

| Step | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Treriksröset → Kilpisjärvi** | Ski **~11–15 km** via Malla / lake · or **Pältsa → Kilpisjärvi ~22 km** |
| **South** | Bus → **Karesuvanto / Karesuando** → **Kiruna** (often overnight) |
| **Home** | **Night train / SJ** from Kiruna south · connect toward NL |

Summer boat does **not** run in April. **Pältsa** may be closed late Apr — [stf-openings](./stf-openings-2027.md).

**Docs:** passport · **EUR + SEK + card** · FI time often **1 h ahead** of SE — check before bus.

### Exit B — Tromsø (Norway)

Ski toward **Goldahytta / Gappohytta** · **Signaldalen** · bus **Tromsø** · bus/train south (or coastal route) toward home.

### Exit C — Bail south

Drop to **Abisko / Björkliden / Riksgränsen** → train south. Contingency only.

### Gear on the way out

| Item | Tip |
|------|-----|
| **Pulk / skis** | Oversized on FI/NO buses — taxi budget · ski bag for long trains |
| **Alkylate left** | Use up or leave — awkward on long public hops |
| **XLite / Ultra** | Abisko ship-home if planned |
