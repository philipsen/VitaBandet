## Gear — companion needs own kit

Assume self-sufficient winter overnight kit (not borrowing your Bandet expedition load).

| Must | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Skis + boots + bindings | BC / touring · tested |
| **Pulk** or heavy pack | Pulk preferred with shared pace |
| Tent **or** share yours | Agree before trip — 2-person pitch vs two tents |
| Sleep system | Bag rated for **−20 °C** nights possible mid-Feb |
| Stove + fuel | Own stove · **alkylate** from car / Grövelsjön (not on bus) |
| Food | **~4–5 days** Grövelsjön → Tänndalen · restock D4 · **~4–5 days** → Storlien |
| Warm layers · mittens · goggles | Mid-Feb fjäll |
| Headlamp · first aid · repair | Personal |
| Phone + power | Offline maps · your GPX segment |
| Cash/card | SE |

**Shared with you (agree):** nav lead (Suunto / GPX) · emergency (inReach on your body) · tent if sharing · cooking some nights.

**Leave in Mora car:** clean clothes for drive home · spare shoes · anything not needed on trail · **fuel bottles if not carried** (companion retrieves for drive — unused alkylate stays with you or in car per plan).

## Exit — Storlien → Mora → NL

### Storlien night (D10 / 24 Feb)

- Book **H**: STF Storliens fjällgård / hotel · or Högfjällshotell  
- Shower · dry kit · pack pulk for **train** (soft bag)  
- Confirm next-day **SJ / Norrtåg** Storlien → Östersund → Mora  
- **Vasalopps** week starts ~**20 Feb** — Mora busy **after** you ski; companion return **25–27 Feb** may hit crowds — book train early

### Train

| | Notes |
|--|-------|
| From | **Storlien** station |
| Toward | **Östersund** then **Mora** (check connections — often **not** one seat) |
| Time | Budget **full travel day** |
| Luggage | Ski bag + packed pulk · fees possible |
| Ticket | [SJ](https://www.sj.se/) · flexible if storm-delayed |

### Car

| | Notes |
|--|-------|
| Collect | Mora long-stay parking · keys + insurance as driver |
| Drive | Mora → NL · winter · **1–2 nights** on road |
| Check-in | Message you on inReach / agreed contact when car is moving |

**Bail options (before Storlien):** Funäsdalen / Tänndalen road · bus toward Mora · skip remaining ski days if injured/weather — car still recoverable.
