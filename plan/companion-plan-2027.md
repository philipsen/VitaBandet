## Gear — companion kit

**Shared (your Bandet kit stays with you after Storlien):** **one tent** · **one stove** · alkylate bottles · nav / inReach.

Companion brings personal ski/pulk/sleep/clothes — **not** a second tent or stove.

| Must | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Skis + boots + bindings | BC / touring · tested |
| **Pulk** or heavy pack | Pulk preferred with shared pace |
| Sleep system | Own bag + mat · rated for **−20 °C** nights possible mid-Feb · fits **shared tent** |
| Food | Own food · cook on **shared stove** · **~4–5 days** Grövelsjön → Tänndalen · restock D4 · **~4–5 days** → Storlien |
| Warm layers · mittens · goggles | Mid-Feb fjäll |
| Headlamp · first aid · repair | Personal |
| Phone + power | Offline maps · your GPX segment |
| Cash/card | SE |

**Do not pack:** second tent · second stove · duplicate cookset (unless tiny mug/bowl only).

**Shared with you:** tent pitch · one stove / cook nights · nav lead (Suunto / GPX) · emergency (inReach on your body).

**Leave in Mora car:** clean clothes for drive home · spare shoes · anything not needed on trail. **Alkylate:** fill from car / Grövelsjön (not on bus) — carried with **shared stove** in your pulk (or split bottles by weight).

## Exit — Storlien → Mora → NL

### Storlien (D10 / 24 Feb)

Night in Storlien after the ski day. Morning train the next day toward Mora.

- Book **H**: STF Storliens fjällgård / hotel · or Högfjällshotell  
- Shower · dry kit · pack pulk for **train** (soft bag)  
- Confirm **morning SJ / Norrtåg** Storlien → Östersund → Mora  
- **Vasalopps** week starts ~**20 Feb** — Mora busy **after** you ski; companion return **25–27 Feb** may hit crowds — book train early

You keep the **shared tent and stove** and continue solo **D11**. Companion leaves with personal kit only.

### B — Hemavan → Ammarnäs (★ hut · ~5–6 days)

Southern **Kungsleden**. Matches the Band line (optional **~7 km E** into Ammarnäs on D39). STF winter on this chain is typically **late Feb → early May** (Viterskalet 2026: **27 Feb – 3 May**) — **open** at your Mar pass.

**PT in:** Länstrafiken Västerbotten **31** Umeå → Hemavan (daily) · or **Hemavan Tärnaby Airport** (Arlanda, ~2 h) · local **320** Hemavan–Tärnaby. [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/) · [visithemavantarnaby.se](https://visithemavantarnaby.se/en/resa-hit/).  
**PT out:** **341** Ammarnäs → Sorsele (~75 min, several/day) → Umeå / inland buses. Night in Ammarnäs (STF Wärdshus) then morning bus.

| Night | Place | Hut? |
|-------|-------|------|
| Before | Hemavan | **STF Hemavan** Fjällstation / Fjällcenter |
| D35 | Viterskalet | **STF Viterskalet** · 32 beds · shop · ~11 km from Hemavan |
| D36 | Syter | **STF Syter** ~1 km off track · 28 beds · no shop |
| D37–D38 | Tärnasjön | **STF Tärnasjö** (detour onto staffed hut) or tent on KL west shore |
| D39 | Ammarnäs | **STF Ammarnäs Wärdshus** · or Fritidscenter camping |

**Also on this KL if you stretch days:** **STF Serve** · **STF Aigert** (Aigert has shop). Summer KL is **~78 km / 6 stages**; winter with pulk is the D35–D39 block.

Companion leaves east by bus; you continue **väster om** on Bandet toward Kvikkjokk.

### C — Ritsem → Abisko (★ hut · ~7 days)

Staffed **Kungsleden** from Ritsem. Your dates **10–17 Apr** sit inside typical KL winter windows ([stf-openings](./stf-openings-2027.md)). **Best public-transport exit on the whole Band** (Malmbanan at Abisko).

**PT in:** Night train → **Gällivare** · **Falcks Omnibus** Gällivare → Ritsem (1–2×/day in season) · book bus **with the train ticket** so connections are protected · [roadtoritsem.com](https://roadtoritsem.com/) · +46 970 240 00. Taxi backup Sverigetaxi Gällivare **0970-10 000** (expensive).  
**PT out:** **Abisko Östra / Abisko Turiststation** — SJ night train south (Kiruna / Stockholm).

| Night | Place | Hut? |
|-------|-------|------|
| D55 | Ritsem | **STF Ritsem** · restaurant · shop · Power Fuel |
| D56 | Sitojaure | **STF Sitojaure** (2026 winter ~27 Feb – 19 Apr) |
| D57 | Hukejaure / Nikkaluokta W | Tent on Band (väster om Nikkaluokta) · optional east: **STF Kebnekaise** / Sarri (off Band) |
| D58 | Sälka | **STF Sälka** |
| D59 | Tjäktja | **STF Tjäktja** (or tent below pass) |
| D60 | Alesjaure | **STF Alesjaure** |
| D61 | Abiskojaure | **STF Abiskojaure** |
| D62 | Abisko | **STF Abisko** year-round · tent site + Keron showers |

Companion can skip Padjelanta and **meet you at Ritsem** (bus in the same day you shop). You keep skiing north together to Abisko.

### D — Hemavan → Kvikkjokk (~12 days)

**PT in:** same as B (Hemavan).  
**PT out:** Länstrafiken Norrbotten **47** Kvikkjokk → Jokkmokk — **weekdays 1×/day** in winter (~05:25 out of Kvikkjokk toward Jokkmokk; reverse ~15:10). No weekend winter 47. Then Jokkmokk → Murjek/Gällivare train. [STF FAQ](https://www.svenskaturistforeningen.se/om-stf/stf-medlemsservice/faq/nar-gar-bussen-till-fran-kvikkjokk/).

| Stretch | Hut? |
|---------|------|
| D35–D39 | Same **STF KL chain** as B (Viterskalet → Ammarnäs) |
| D40–D46 | **Mostly tent** on KL west. Optional **H** detours (off Band, east): **Vuoggatjålme Fjällhotell** (~21 km) · **Adolfström** camping/stugor (~7 km) · **Jäckvik** Kyrkans Fjällgård / ICA (VGB: pass väster om) |
| D47 | **STF Kvikkjokk** (phone ahead — no 2027 block on STF page) · no tent field |

Harder than B/C: long food carry, ice on Hornavan, thin exit bus. Hut-heavy only if they **quit at Ammarnäs** (option B).

### E — Kvikkjokk → Abisko (~15 days)

**PT:** bus **47** in · Abisko train out.

On **your** line this is **Padjelanta-west tent** (D48–D54) then the **C** hut chain from Ritsem. Padjelanta STF/Sámi stugor exist off the west line — do not count on them. Companion who wants huts should **join at Ritsem** (C), not at Kvikkjokk.

### F — Abisko → Kilpisjärvi / Treriksröset (~8 days)

**PT in:** Abisko train (easiest gate on the Band).  
**PT out:** no road at the cairn — ski to **Kilpisjärvi** then Matkahuolto buses toward Karesuvanto / Kiruna (fragile Apr timetable — see [transport](./transport-considerations-2027.md)). Tromsø via Signaldalen is the Norway alt.

### G — Tänndalen → Storlien (short join onto A)

Companion skips Grövelsjön and meets you at **Hamra Livs (D4)**. **PT in:** Härjedalingen / Länstrafiken to **Funäsdalen** · winter ski bus to Tänndalen · Snälltåget + Östersund transfer (selected days). **PT out:** Storlien train (same as A).

Huts: village lodging D4 + Storlien D10; **Helags / Sylarna / Blåhammaren still closed**. Shared tent for D5–D9.
