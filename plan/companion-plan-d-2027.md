## Gear — companion kit

Staffed huts are the plan. A tent is only the Tjäktja bail (below the pass) or a storm night.

| Must | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Skis + boots + bindings | BC / touring · tested |
| **Pulk** or pack | Food and sleep kit for the hut chain |
| Sleep system | Own bag + mat · STF bunk |
| Food | From the **Ritsem** shop for the leg to Abisko |
| Warm layers · mittens · goggles | April, still fjäll |
| Headlamp · first aid · repair | Personal |
| Phone + power | Offline maps |
| Cash/card | SE · STF member card if they have one |

**Shared with you:** nav lead · emergency (inReach on your body). They do not need a stove, cooking in the huts.

---

## Food & fuel

Shop at **STF Ritsem** on the meet day (restaurant · shop · Power Fuel). Hut shops along the KL are top-ups, not the plan. Abisko is the exit, not a trail resupply for them.

---

## Exit — Abisko

- **STF Abisko** · shower at Keron  
- **Abisko Östra / Abisko Turiststation** — SJ night train south  
- Pack pulk for the train (soft bag)

You continue solo north from Abisko with your own kit. Companion leaves with personal kit only.

**Bail:** Sverigetaxi Gällivare **0970-10 000** if the Falcks bus is missed (expensive). Once on the KL, the easy exit is Abisko.
