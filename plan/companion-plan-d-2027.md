# Companion plan D — Ritsem → Abisko · Apr 2027

Two companions. They are at **STF Ritsem on Saturday 3 April**, ski the staffed hut chain to **Abisko** (~121 km), and take the night train south.

**Sunday 4 April** is the spare day. It absorbs a weather delay on the approach. When the approach is on time, that day is the shop and the rest, and the ski north starts **Monday 5 April**.

## Beds — six nights, two people

They book **two beds** for these six nights. Mountain huts only. **Ritsem** and **Abisko turiststation** stay out of this booking: those are fjällstationer, and a full refund there stops **7 days** before arrival.

Fjällstugor refund in full until **18:00 the day before** arrival. Cancel in **Min bokning**. A booking made on the arrival day is binding, and online booking closes at that 18:00. Be at the hut by **18:00** or the guaranteed bed drops to whatever bunk is left.

| Night | Date | Hut |
|------:|------|-----|
| 1 | Mon 5 Apr | **STF Sitojaure** |
| 2 | Tue 6 Apr | **STF Hukejaure** |
| 3 | Wed 7 Apr | **STF Sälka** |
| 4 | Thu 8 Apr | **STF Tjäktja** |
| 5 | Fri 9 Apr | **STF Alesjaure** |
| 6 | Sat 10 Apr | **STF Abiskojaure** |

Your own bunk is a separate booking. If the approach uses the spare day, these six nights still hold. If a later storm slips a night, cancel that hut before 18:00 the day before. STF also moves a fjällstuga bed to another hut during the trip when weather changes the line.

## Exit — Abisko

- **STF Abisko** on **Sun 11 Apr** · shower at Keron
- Train south from **Abisko Östra / Abisko Turiststation**
- Pack for the train

You leave **Mon 12 Apr** toward Lappjordhytta with your own kit.

**Early exit, from Hukejaure.** Reverse to Ritsem: Hukejaure → Sitojaure (19 km) → Ritsem (22 km). Falcks back to Gällivare (**13:30 → 17:30** on the winter sheet). Cancel the unused huts before 18:00 the day before.

**From Sälka, east.** **STF Singi** (~12 km), then **STF Kebnekaise** (~14 km, open **26 Feb – 18 Apr 2027**), then ~19 km to **Nikkaluokta** or a booked snowmobile. [Nikkaluoktaexpressen](https://nikkaluoktaexpressen.se/) to Kiruna, then the train. This is the corridor the line otherwise stays west of.

Once over Tjäktja, Abisko is the simpler way out.
