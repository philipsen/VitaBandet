# Companion plan C — Hemavan → Kvikkjokk · Klaartje · Mar 2027

**Working later-leg for Klaartje.** Meet on the Hemavan overnight (no rest day), ski the Kungsleden-west / Hornavan corridor to Kvikkjokk, bus out. Shared tent + stove (same model as [A](./companion-plan-2027.md)). Does **not** recover the Mora car.

**Shorter hut exit:** quit at **Ammarnäs** on D39 (~**70 km**, ~5–6 days) and take bus **341** to Sorsele. Staffed Kungsleden the whole way — see [Ammarnäs exit](#ammarnäs-exit--staffed-huts). You continue **väster om** toward Kvikkjokk.

**PT in:** Länstrafiken Västerbotten **31** Umeå → Hemavan (daily) · or **Hemavan Tärnaby Airport** (Arlanda, ~2 h) · local **320** Hemavan–Tärnaby. [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/) · [visithemavantarnaby.se](https://visithemavantarnaby.se/en/resa-hit/). Night **STF Hemavan** / Fjällcenter on the arrive night (**D34 · Mon 15 Mar**). You ski out together **Tue 16 Mar**.  
**PT out (Kvikkjokk):** Länstrafiken Norrbotten **47** Kvikkjokk kyrkan → Jokkmokk busstation — winter/low season **Mon–Fri 05:25** only (Jokkmokk ~07:45; reverse **15:10**). **No Sat/Sun trip** in the current low-season timetable. Then Jokkmokk → Murjek/Gällivare train. [STF FAQ](https://www.svenskaturistforeningen.se/om-stf/stf-medlemsservice/faq/nar-gar-bussen-till-fran-kvikkjokk/) · [helgtrafik](https://lanstrafikennorrbotten.se/helgtrafik).

Arrival **Thu 25 Mar** is **skärtorsdag** (a normal Thursday). The exit window is **påsk 2027** (påskdagen **Sun 28 Mar**). Länstrafiken runs **långfredag** and **annandag påsk** as a **Sunday**, and **påskafton** as a **Saturday**. With no weekend 47, there is **no bus 26–29 Mar**.

2027 winter timetable is not published yet — recheck the [reseplanerare](https://lanstrafikennorrbotten.se/) before booking. Taxi Jokkmokk is the expensive bail off this pattern.

**PT out (Ammarnäs):** **341** Ammarnäs → Sorsele (~75 min, several/day) → Umeå / inland buses. That quit is **Sat 20 Mar**, the weekend **before** påsk. Night in Ammarnäs (STF Wärdshus) then morning bus.

## Exit — Kvikkjokk or Ammarnäs

### Kvikkjokk (D44 / Thu 25 Mar)

- **STF Kvikkjokk** (phone ahead — no 2027 block on STF page) · no tent field  
- Stay through **påsk**. First **47** is **Tue 30 Mar 05:25** Kvikkjokk kyrkan → Jokkmokk ~07:45  
- Then Jokkmokk → Murjek/Gällivare train

You keep the **shared tent and stove** and continue solo from **D45**.

### Ammarnäs exit — staffed huts

Southern **Kungsleden**. Matches the Band line (optional **~7 km E** into Ammarnäs on D39). STF winter on this chain is typically **late Feb → early May** (Viterskalet 2026: **27 Feb – 3 May**) — **open** at the Mar pass.

| Night | Place | Hut? |
|-------|-------|------|
| Before | Hemavan | **STF Hemavan** Fjällstation / Fjällcenter |
| D35 | Viterskalet | **STF Viterskalet** · 32 beds · shop · ~11 km from Hemavan |
| D36 | Syter | **STF Syter** ~1 km off track · 28 beds · no shop |
| D37–D38 | Tärnasjön | **STF Tärnasjö** (detour onto staffed hut) or tent on KL west shore |
| D39 | Ammarnäs | **STF Ammarnäs Wärdshus** · or Fritidscenter camping · bus **341** |

**Also on this KL if you stretch days:** **STF Serve** · **STF Aigert** (Aigert has shop). Summer KL is **~78 km / 6 stages**; winter with pulk is the D35–D39 block.

She leaves east by bus; you continue **väster om** on Bandet toward Kvikkjokk.

| Stretch | Hut? |
