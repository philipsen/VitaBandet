# Companion plan C — Hemavan → Kvikkjokk · Klaartje · Mar 2027

**Working later-leg for Klaartje.** She arrives in Hemavan on the rest day, then skis the Kungsleden huts through Ammarnäs and on to Kvikkjokk. Shared tent + stove after the huts (same model as [A](./companion-plan-2027.md)). Does **not** recover the Mora car.

**PT in:** SJ night train into Umeå **07:11**, then Länstrafiken **bus 31** Umeå busstation **07:30** → Hemavan köpcentrum about **13:05** on **Sunday 14 Mar**. You are already in Hemavan from **Saturday 13 Mar** (shop). Sunday is the rest day. Ski out **Monday 15 Mar**. March 2027 times are not published yet. [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/).  
**PT out (Kvikkjokk):** Länstrafiken Norrbotten **47** Kvikkjokk kyrkan → Jokkmokk busstation — winter/low season **Mon–Fri 05:25** only (Jokkmokk ~07:45; reverse **15:10**). **No Sat/Sun trip** in the current low-season timetable. Then Jokkmokk → Murjek/Gällivare train. [STF FAQ](https://www.svenskaturistforeningen.se/om-stf/stf-medlemsservice/faq/nar-gar-bussen-till-fran-kvikkjokk/) · [helgtrafik](https://lanstrafikennorrbotten.se/helgtrafik).

Arrival **Sat 27 Mar** is **påskafton**. Länstrafiken runs **långfredag** and **annandag påsk** as a **Sunday**, and **påskafton** as a **Saturday**. With no weekend 47, there is **no bus 26–29 Mar**.

| Date | Day | Bus 47 out |

| Sat 27 Mar | Påskafton · arrive after the ski | **No bus** |
| Sun 28 Mar | Påskdagen · rest | **No bus** |
| Mon 29 Mar | **Annandag påsk** · as Sunday | **No bus** |
| **Tue 30 Mar** | Normal weekday | **05:25** · first bus |

2027 winter timetable is not published yet — recheck the [reseplanerare](https://lanstrafikennorrbotten.se/) before booking. Taxi Jokkmokk is the expensive bail off this pattern.

**PT out (Ammarnäs):** **341** Ammarnäs → Sorsele (~75 min) → Umeå / inland buses. Night **Fri 19 Mar** at STF Wärdshus, morning bus **Saturday 20 Mar**.
